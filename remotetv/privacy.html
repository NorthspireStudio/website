RemoteTV Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Northspire Studio · contact@northspirestudio.com

RemoteTV is an Android app that turns your phone into a remote control for your TV, over your home Wi-Fi network or the phone's infrared (IR) blaster. It is made by Northspire Studio ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: RemoteTV has no account and no servers of its own. Your TVs and settings stay on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Information stored only on your phone

To work as a remote, the app saves the following on your phone:

  • The TVs you add: the name you give them, the TV type, their address on your local network, and, for some TVs, their network hardware (MAC) address, used to switch the TV on over Wi-Fi.
  • The pairing keys and access tokens that TVs issue when you pair the app with them.
  • IR codes for TVs you set up with the IR blaster.
  • Your app settings, the time the last ad was shown, and whether you have an active subscription.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is excluded from Android cloud backups. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

2. Communication with your TV

RemoteTV finds TVs on your local network and talks to them directly, using the control methods each TV maker provides. These connections stay within your home network and never pass through our servers. The IR blaster sends infrared light to the TV and does not involve any data.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows an ad from Google AdMob when you open the app, at most once every 10 minutes. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived,
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system version and language,
  • interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's privacy policy. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in RemoteTV under Settings › Ad privacy choices. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).

Subscribers to "Remove ads" see no ads: the app does not request ads for them.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

The "Remove ads" subscription is sold and processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of whether your Google account has an active subscription, so it can switch ads off. You can manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Feedback emails

If you use "Report a bug" or "Suggest an improvement", the app opens your own email app with a draft. Nothing is sent until you press Send. A bug report draft includes the app version, your phone model, Android version and the type of TV connection, all visible for you to edit. When you email us, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to reply and to fix problems, we do not share it, and we delete it on request.

6. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet, network state, Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast: to find and control TVs on your network, and to load ads.
  • Infrared transmitter: to control TVs with the IR blaster, on phones that have one.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as described in section 3.
  • Google Play billing: to offer the "Remove ads" subscription.

RemoteTV does not access your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone. The only third party involved is Google, through AdMob and Google Play, as described above. We do not use analytics or tracking services of our own.

8. Children

RemoteTV is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Security

Information the app stores stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Connections to TVs use the TV maker's own protocols; some are encrypted (for example Android TV and Google TV), while others, defined by the TV maker, are not encrypted but stay within your local network.

10. Your choices

  • Delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it or clearing its data in Android settings.
  • Change ad consent in Settings › Ad privacy choices (where offered), and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Remove ads entirely with the "Remove ads" subscription.
  • Ask us to delete emails you sent us by writing to contact@northspirestudio.com.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
